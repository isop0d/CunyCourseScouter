Frequently asked questions
Quick answers about search, statuses, and ratings.
What do the Open, Waitlist, and Closed statuses mean? −
Open means seats are available to register now. Waitlist means the section is full but you can join a queue for a seat. Closed means no seats or waitlist spots remain.
How often is seat availability updated? +
Section status and seat counts refresh periodically throughout the day, pulled directly from the registrar's system. Open seats can fill quickly — refresh the page if you're planning to register.
Where do the RMP ratings come from? +
Ratings are pulled from Rate My Professors and shown as an average score with the number of reviews in parentheses. A dash means no rating is on file for that instructor.
How is the rating color determined? +
Green (≥ 4.2) indicates a strong average, amber (3.3–4.19) a mixed one, and red (< 3.3) a low one. Colors are intentionally muted so they're easy to scan without feeling alarming.
Can I save a schedule across visits? +
Yes — log in with Discord and any class you add on the Schedule page saves to your account instantly, so your schedule persists across visits and devices.
Does the search look at instructor names too? +
Yes, the search box matches against course code, course title, and instructor name all at once. Try typing a last name to find all sections taught by that instructor.
What does the Watch feature do? +
Watching a section sends you a Discord notification when its status changes — for example when a closed section opens up or when a waitlisted section gets a seat. Watching and your Schedule are independent: watch a class for alerts, and add whichever ones you like to your Schedule. On the Schedule page your watched classes appear in a "Watched" dropdown so you can add any of them in one click. Login with Discord to enable watching.
